This project analyzes store sales performance and customer purchasing behavior using Python.
The analysis integrates customer, product, order, order-item, payment, shipment, and return datasets.
The cleaned fact table contains 37,501 order-item records and 15,000 distinct orders.
Gross line revenue reached ₱7,378,253.06, with an average order value of ₱491.88.
The analysis covers monthly sales trends from January 2024 to January 2026.
April 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue, while February 2025 recorded the lowest.
Toys generated the highest recognized category revenue, followed by Electronics.
Central Luzon recorded the highest revenue among the six regions analyzed.
Repeat customers represented 84.1% of identified customers under the selected-period definition.
The dashboard presents sales KPIs, product and regional performance, customer spending, and cancellation trends.
